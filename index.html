New Glassdoor jobs from the last 24 hours appear here after each Gmail scan, with a fit score against your CV and UK sponsorship checks.

How this works. Every morning Claude reads your Glassdoor alerts, keeps only jobs posted in the last 24 hours, scores each one against your master CV, and checks the employer on the Home Office register of licensed sponsors. Buttons open a pre-filled GitHub issue: tap Submit and a GitHub Action records it within a minute. For jobs you approve, Claude writes a tailored Word CV at the next scan and emails it to you. When an "application received" email lands, the job moves to Applied.

Sponsorship checks: Skilled Worker general threshold £41,700; new-entrant rate £33,400. A licensed sponsor can still decline to sponsor a given role, so read the advert.
