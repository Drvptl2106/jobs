# Master CV

Upload your master CV here as **`master_cv.pdf`** (or `master_cv.docx`).

Upload page: https://github.com/Drvptl2106/jobs/upload/claude/ecstatic-bardeen-eu42vz/cv
(open it in a browser while signed in to GitHub, choose the file, then **Commit changes**).

Format:
- A text-based PDF exported from Word or Google Docs, or a .docx. Not a scanned image or photo.
- Your full "master" version: every job, project and skill, with details and numbers. Longer than one
  page is fine; Claude picks what fits each job.
- Leave out your phone number and home address: this repo and the Job Desk website are public.

Claude converts it to `master_cv.md` and uses only facts from it when scoring jobs and writing
tailored CVs. Upload a new copy (same name) whenever your CV changes.
